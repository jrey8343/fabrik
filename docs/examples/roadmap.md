# Roadmap

## Now

- Patients join a waitlist for a fully booked day, and get a text when a slot comes free.
- Clinic owners see the day's cancellations, and who took each freed slot.

## Next

- Patients rebook their usual treatment in one tap from the reminder text.
- Clinic owners block out a room for an afternoon without cancelling anyone.

## Later

- Clinics with more than one practitioner share one waitlist.
- Patients choose a practitioner as well as a time.
